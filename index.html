Privacy Policy

How LIANGLUI KHUANG handles information and protects your privacy.

Last updated: September 2026

LIANGLUI KHUANG is a digital songbook developed by Wingambou Newmai.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the LIANGLUI KHUANG application.

1. Information We Collect

LIANGLUI KHUANG does not require you to create an account and does not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, email address, telephone number, contacts, precise location, photographs, microphone recordings, or account credentials.

The application does not use advertising or analytics services to build a profile about users.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Some application preferences and songbook-related information are stored locally on your device using the application's local storage. This information is used to provide features and remember your preferences.

This may include:

  • Reading preferences such as theme, font, font size, line spacing, and text alignment.
  • Preferences controlling which song information is displayed.
  • Your favourite song selections.
  • Your recently viewed song information, such as the collection, song ID, title, and translation.

This locally stored information is not used to identify you and is not intentionally transmitted by LIANGLUI KHUANG to a remote personal-data server.

3. Song and Application Data

The songbook content used by the application is provided as part of the application's data. The application may load this content locally while you browse songs, categories, collections, search results, and favourites.

4. Internet and Third-Party Services

The Android application includes Internet access because it is a web-based application running inside a native Android wrapper. The application code uses local application paths to load its songbook data.

LIANGLUI KHUANG does not use an advertising network, analytics platform, user-account server, or cloud database for the application's core songbook features.

The application also provides a "Rate this App" feature. When you choose to use it, the application requests the platform's native in-app review interface. Review handling is subject to the policies and practices of the relevant app store platform.

5. Data Sharing

LIANGLUI KHUANG does not intentionally sell, rent, or share your locally stored favourites, reading preferences, or recently viewed song information with third parties.

6. Data Security

Information stored by the application for preferences and songbook- related features remains in the application's local storage on your device. You should use the normal security protections provided by your device, including an appropriate screen lock.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored application preferences and songbook-related information remain on your device until they are changed, cleared, or removed by the application or by you.

You can remove the application's locally stored information by using the available reset/clear features within the application where provided, or by clearing the application's data through your Android device settings.

8. Children's Privacy

LIANGLUI KHUANG does not knowingly collect personal information from children or require users to provide personal information to use the core songbook features.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application, functionality, or applicable requirements change. The updated policy will be made available within the application.
